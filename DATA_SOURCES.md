## Image policy

Event, venue, promo, and nightlife thumbnails use images served by the represented businesses’ official sites. Restaurant cards use food photography instead of logos. Commis, Burdell, Mago, Parche, FOB Kitchen, Belotti, and Pizzaiolo use photography from their official sites; Aburaya uses a reusable karaage image from Wikimedia Commons. Expanded-directory rows use representative editorial food photography from Unsplash and do not present those images as a specific restaurant dish. The khao soi and Korean-barbecue thumbnails are Creative Commons images routed through Wikimedia Commons. `Photo` retains the striped fallback when a remote host is unavailable.
